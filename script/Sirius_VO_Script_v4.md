## Script

Delivering outcomes requires people, processes, data, systems and controls working together seamlessly.

As customer expectations, business priorities and ways of working evolve at NatWest, we need a more connected and adaptable way of working.

Sirius enables that shift, creating a connected, AI-enabled environment where work can be set up, managed and continuously improved.

Getting started with Sirius is simple.

Workflow Studio puts teams in control, making it easy to introduce new business processes or adapt existing ones as requirements evolve.

Define how work should flow, configure necessary validations, controls and exception paths, and move rapidly from design to execution using intelligent recommendations and reusable components.

Integration Hub enables those workflows to connect with the channels, systems and data they need, bringing the right information into the process where and when it is needed.

Once up and running, workflows can be viewed in action through Single Window.

Gain visibility into how work is progressing across the process and quickly identify what requires manual attention.

AI-generated summaries bring explainability to workflows and help teams triage and resolve exceptions more effectively, while complete case history, supporting context and operational insights enable confident decision-making.

With work and context brought together in a unified view, teams can coordinate more effectively, reduce delays and keep outcomes on track.

Alongside managing the work itself, teams can understand what is happening across the broader process.

Observability brings together signals from across processes, systems and data to provide real-time visibility into overall performance, helping teams identify bottlenecks in workflows, spot emerging issues early, and continuously improve how work gets done.

Across every stage of this journey, intelligence is embedded throughout Sirius.

From automating business checks, highlighting exceptions and providing intelligent recommendations, to copilots that help users find information, understand context and determine the next best action, AI helps teams make more informed decisions and act with greater confidence.

Bringing all of this together in Sirius creates a more self-service and flexible way of working, giving teams greater control to configure, adapt and evolve how work gets done as needs change.

That means stronger control and more time for colleagues to focus on higher-value work, creating better experiences and outcomes for customers and colleagues alike.
